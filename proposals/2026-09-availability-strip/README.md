# S3 Proposal: Availability Strip Leading the Hut Body (2026-09)

On the hut detail panel, availability — the product's core signal — sits one interaction deep behind the date chip while the weather briefing leads the body. This mockup compares three ways to surface availability directly under the hut description, each in Day and Night, using the approved 7-state occupancy scale (free #25bf5e, free_unknown #7fab88, low #f6ad4b, medium #ea9a37, high #c3731f, full #961a17, unknown #575757) with theme-correct text shades and every state paired with a count or label, never color alone: **(A) 7-Day Mini Strip** of seven day tiles (day label + occupancy dot + free count, selected day = tonal fill + inset gold ring, tap opens the calendar); **(B) Month Heat Strip** — a compact month row of tiny state-colored cells with today's gold inset, rounded weekend ticks and a "next free night" summary line; **(C) Summary Row Only** — one line of the next free nights with mini occupancy dots plus a forest-green "Kalender" button.

**Recommendation: Variant A (7-Day Mini Strip)** for the detail panel. It answers the go/no-go question ("when does my group fit?") at a glance, exercises the full scale including free_unknown in real context, and keeps a clean hand-off to the calendar for longer horizons. Variant B is the better planning instrument but is the smallest tap-target and the most color-heavy block in the panel — better suited inside the calendar view than as the strip; Variant C is the right fallback for very small viewports or huts with sparse data, and its "next free" phrasing is worth keeping as the sheet's summary line in any variant.
